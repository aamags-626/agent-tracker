## The Book

**Title:** *Shamediary*
**Form:** Fragmented, essayistic literary memoir. Non-linear. Short titled sections. Uses found objects (police report, letters, disbarment doc, postcards) as structural anchors.
**Length:** ~65–70k words (estimated)

**What it's about:** Exploring the body, death, and grief in a constellation of fragments. Queer and queerying — about avoidance and investigation. A narrator peeling back layers of self through two defining experiences: her father's death (at 17 — he fell drunk, hit his head; her reaction was numbness, relief) and her first queer relationship (Noa).

**What it's really about:** The same thing — avoidance and investigation. The book doesn't resolve, it accumulates. It sits with what can't be named cleanly.

**Narrator:** Unnamed first person ("I"). Non-linear structure moves between NOW (present/therapy), Death + [days], and deep past.

**Voice:** Precise, wry, clinical at moments, quietly devastating. Never sentimental.

**Comp titles (working):**
- *The Argonauts* — Maggie Nelson (2015) — establishes the formal/queer/essayistic lineage; foundational to the conversation this book is in
- *Negative Space* — Lilly Dancyger (2021) — fragmented memoir about father's death using found objects as structural anchors; closest structural/subject overlap
- *Small Rain* — Garth Greenwell (2024) — queer, body-forward, lyric; most recent comp, signals the book is in the current literary conversation

## Query Letter

The query letter does not exist yet — it needs to be drafted. Key constraints:
- The book resists easy summary — the query needs to honor the form without making it sound inaccessible
- Lead with specificity and voice, not vague literary gesture
- Bio is strong — the publication record and editorial role belong in the query
- Comps are locked: Nelson (lineage), Dancyger (structural precision), Greenwell (currency). Nelson is older but defensible given Al's credentials and how foundational it is to this conversation.

When drafting or refining the query, check against standard agent expectations: hook, book summary (~1 paragraph), comps, bio, word count + genre, manuscript status.
