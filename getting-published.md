## Resources

### Finding Agents
- [QueryTracker](https://querytracker.net) — search and track agents by genre
- [Manuscript Wishlist (MSWL)](https://www.manuscriptwishlist.com) — agents post what they're looking for
- [Publishers Marketplace](https://publishersmarketplace.com) — deal tracking, agent profiles (paid)
- **Start with comp authors** — look up who reps Melissa Febos and Maggie Nelson, then explore their agencymates

### Criteria
- US-based
- Boutique agency
- Reps literary memoir and/or autofiction

### Submission Materials

#### Query Letter
- Hook (1–2 sentences)
- Book summary (1 paragraph)
- Comp titles
- Bio (brief, relevant)
- Word count + genre
- Status: [ ] Drafted [ ] Revised [ ] Ready

#### Synopsis
- 1–2 page overview of full narrative arc
- Status: [ ] Drafted [ ] Revised [ ] Ready

#### Sample Pages
- Typically first 10–50 pages (varies by agent)
- Status: [ ] Drafted [ ] Revised [ ] Ready

#### Notes
- Always check each agent's specific submission guidelines before querying
